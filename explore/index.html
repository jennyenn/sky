Options to choose from

Explorations for the home page and a new process page. They use the real data, photos and sketches. Pick one of each (or mix) and it gets built into the site.

Home page

A · Sky strips

The whole year as 75 thin gradient strips in date order. Hover a strip to see its photo, date and mood.

 B · Full sky

The sky fills the screen and slowly changes. Press and hold to see the real photo behind it, from your notebook sketch.

 C · Sky title

A huge "Sky Diary" title with the sky moving through the letters, plus which sky it is.

Process page

A · Diary

Scrolls down like diary pages: polaroids, photo-to-color pairs, taped sketches, references. Four numbered steps.

 B · Sideways

One long sideways canvas like the mood page, with your process boards and sketches laid out like stickers.

 C · Transform

All 75 skies in a grid that turn from photo, to colors, to their mood shape. Sketches below.
